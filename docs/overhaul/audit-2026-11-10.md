# Live-art audit, 2026-11-10 (Josh: Chase Center and Petco Park are the bar)

Rules applied to all 35 live venues:
1. **Art bar**: must match Chase Center / Petco Park (crisp, whole, clean edges, no artifacts). Blocky, voxel-like or brick-like is an automatic fail.
2. **Model path**: any image that traces back to the 3D-model path is pulled.
3. **Frame (new QA check 5, `scripts/stadiums/overhaul/margin_check.py`)**: the whole venue must be in frame, with at least 5% margin on every side. Checked in two places:
   - the venue's mask inside its **reference photo** (where cut-offs start: Petco's photo cuts the stadium on the right);
   - the generated cutout must not touch its canvas edge, and the **shipped** hero and sprites are padded to at least 5% margin on every side.

## Brick assets
- `public/stadiums/atlas/` (old brick sprite sheets, unreferenced but still deployed and publicly reachable) removed.
- Brick heroes, sprites and GLB models were removed in the 2026-11-10 release; their old URLs now return the app shell, not an image.
- No service worker is registered. Static assets are served `cache-control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`, so browsers revalidate and the CDN serves only the current deployment.

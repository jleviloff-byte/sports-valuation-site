## Method notes

- Forbes at time of sale = the latest Forbes list for that league published before the announcement date, taken from the Forbes values in the site data (or the owner's Forbes export once loaded). Where a newer Forbes list exists but is not yet loaded, the deal is marked stale in `forbesSource`.
- Premium to Forbes = valuation / Forbes at sale - 1. For minority deals the valuation is the implied whole-team mark.
- Currency conversions use the rate cited by the source or the spot rate on the announcement date (noted per deal in research/raw).
